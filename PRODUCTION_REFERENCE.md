## About Us page (verbatim copy from production)

Hero / Where it started:
> It started the way most real things do — not with a business plan, not with investors, not with a launch strategy. It started with a garage door half-open, the smell of pressed cotton, and the Bay Area fog rolling in at dusk.
>
> We weren't trying to build a brand. We were just trying to make something we actually wanted to wear. Something that felt like *us*. Something that said where we came from without having to say a word.
>
> Because California isn't just a place — it's something you carry in you. The way the late afternoon light hits the Bay Bridge. The way Oakland hits different after dark. The way San Francisco smells like salt and ambition all at once.
>
> You can leave California. California doesn't leave you. So we stitched it into a hat.

Pull-quote:
> "We're not selling headwear. We're selling a feeling — and every Californian already knows exactly what that feels like." — CALIFORNIA HAT HOUSE

"How We Built This" three pillars:
- **The Garage Years** — No shortcuts. No manufacturers handed to us. We figured out every stitch, every color match, every trucker mesh weight the hard way — order samples, test them, hate them, try again. The Bay Area taught us one thing: if you can't build it yourself, you don't really know what you're building.
- **City First, Always** — SF. Oakland. LA. San Diego. These aren't just cities to us — they're personalities, histories, attitudes. Every colorway, every embroidery placement, every design decision runs through one filter: does this feel like that city? If we have to ask, the answer is no.
- **Real People, Real Pride** — We didn't grow this with ads. We grew it one person at a time — people who put it on and immediately understood. That's still the test. If you put on a CHH hat and something clicks — some wordless recognition of where you're from — then we did our job right.

Signal section:
> **It's Not A Hat. It's A Signal.**
> When someone across the room sees your CHH hat, they don't think "nice hat." They think — that person is from somewhere real. Somewhere with culture. With coastline. With something to say.
>
> That moment of recognition? That's what we've been building since day one in that garage. Not a product. A conversation starter. An identity you wear.

Mood tags:
`Golden Hour` · `Bay Fog` · `405 at Midnight` · `Taco Truck Fridays` · `Oakland Grit` · `SD Slow Life`

Closing block:
> The California State of Mind. **Born on the Coast. Built for Every Head.** No team. No league. No permission needed.
>
> **Rep Your City.** Because where you're from is worth wearing.
